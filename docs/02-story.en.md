## 1. One line

A new process engineer's first year in a wafer fab. Chasing yield by day, fixing tools at night, and trying to stay whole while a manager works through every manipulation tactic in the book.

## 2. Theme and stance

**Theme:** Workplace manipulation (known in Chinese-speaking workplaces as "workplace PUA") gets passed down. The director squeezes the manager, the manager squeezes you, and a year later you'll have a new hire of your own. The game asks one question: when it's your turn, will you be the one who stops it?

**Stance:**
- Manipulation is the enemy's move set, never a reward for the player. The player grows by learning to **see it, record it, set boundaries, and find allies**.
- "The Cycle" is a bad ending. The game never rewards the player for manipulating someone else.
- Manager Wang is not a pure villain. The system squeezes him too, and he has his own fears.
- Tone: grounded realism with dark humor. The jokes come from insider recognition, never from mocking the person on the receiving end.

## 3. World

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Company | Xijing Semiconductor (fictional), Fab 9, a 300 mm fab running 24/7 |
| Your department | Process Integration · ETCH Process Section (PE) |
| Time frame | One year, from your first day on Mon 2026-08-24 until the next new hire arrives the following August |
| Shifts | 12-hour day and night shifts, plus on-call |
| Language | Everyday Taiwanese workplace speech. Fab jargon stays as-is (OOC, Hold, PM, FDC, Recipe, tool hand-over) |

## 5. Prologue: Day One

**Day 1 · Mon 2026-08-24**

The prologue doubles as the tutorial. It teaches movement, interaction, asking people, tactic recognition, and the evidence log.

### S1 · 07:20 Security gate

- You pick up a temporary badge. The guard covers your phone camera with a tamper sticker: "Every day, in and out. Peel it and you get a demerit."
- **Tutorial:** moving and talking to NPCs.

### S2 · 08:00 Orientation room

- Amy plays the safety videos: chemicals, emergency showers, earthquake evacuation. Then the infosec rules and the company values: "Integrity, Innovation, Accountability."
- You raise your hand: "So who teaches us the process side?"
- **Amy:** "Your manager will teach you the technical stuff in your unit."
- You leave with a 300-page New Hire Handbook. Not one page is about your actual job.

### S3 · 10:30 Process section office

- Manager Wang is in a meeting. Your desk has a monitor but no PC; your account takes three working days.
- **Kevin** (without looking up): "Start with the SOPs."
- The shared drive holds 2,000 pages of SOPs. The newest file is named `ETCH_SOP_final_v3_new(2)_edited.pdf`.

### S4 · 11:45 Meeting Manager Wang

> **Manager Wang:** "Welcome, welcome. This isn't school. Nobody's going to hold your hand. If you don't know something, go ask. Be proactive."
> **Manager Wang:** "Come to lunch with everyone. We're one big family here."

- [Tactic setup: Double bind (first half), We're a family]

### S5 · 13:30 The "Ask Someone" minigame

**Task:** figure out what "holding a lot" means. You can ask three people:

| Who you ask | Result |
|---|---|
| Kevin | "Chapter 3 of the SOP." (He's rushing a report.) → Kevin's patience − |
| Manager Wang | "You need to ask about *this*? Did you even look it up first?" → Sanity −, [Double bind triggered] |
| Fang-jie (you have to walk to the technicians' break room) | She shows you a real hold and release on the MES screen, and adds: "Before you ask someone, tell them what you already checked." → Unlocks the "Asking Well" skill |

- **Lesson:** ask the right person, the right way. From here on, every "ask" option gains a "Say what I already checked" variant with a better success rate.

### S6 · 15:00 Section meeting

> **Manager Wang:** "New hire. Tell us what's wrong with this SPC chart."

- You get it half right.

> **Manager Wang:** "You don't know *this*? What did they teach you in school?"
> **Manager Wang:** (turning away) "Kevin could read these in his first week."

- [Tactics: Personal attack, Comparison]
- **Tutorial:** Tactic Recognition appears for the first time. A prompt flickers at the screen edge: "? Something about that line felt off." The player can tag the line with a card from the Tactics Codex, with guidance the first time. A correct tag halves the sanity loss.

### S7 · 18:30 End of the workday

- Nobody in the office stands up. Manager Wang walks behind your chair:

> **Manager Wang:** "New hires don't need to rush off. Stay and learn a bit."

- [Tactics: For your own good, unpaid overtime]
- **Choice:**
  - Stay → Sanity −, Wang's approval +
  - "I'll head home and organize my notes. I'll be in at 7:30 tomorrow." → Boundary set. Wang's face tightens, but he says nothing. Sanity holds

### S8 · 23:10 Your rented room

- A LINE group invite pops up: "**Fab9 ETCH One Big Happy Family**".
- Manager Wang's message in the group:

> **Manager Wang:** "8 a.m. stand-up tomorrow. New hire gives a 5-minute self-intro. With data."

- [Tactic: last-minute demand]
- Narration:

> Day one. You learned three things: if you don't know, ask. If you ask, you get scolded. If you don't ask, something breaks.

- **Evidence Log unlocked:** you can save today's conversations as timestamped records. This log will save you in Chapters 2 and 6.

## 6. Chapter outlines

Each chapter weaves a tech case (gameplay) with a manipulation theme (story).

| Ch. | When | Tech case | Manipulation theme | Playable as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue | 2026-08-24 | Tutorial | Double bind, We're a family, Personal attack, Comparison | PE |
| 1 | Week 2 | Case 01: false OOC from a drifting metrology tool | Criticism without standards, Moving the goalposts, For your own good | PE |
| 2 | Night of 10/21 | Case 03: ETCH-07 focus ring (the current prototype) | Credit stealing, Gaslighting | PE |
| 3 | Nov–Dec | Particle excursion; PE and EE blame each other | Triangulation | PE + EE (Bear) |
| 4 | Jan–Feb | New product yield ramp; year-end party | Empty promises, Comparison | PE |
| 5 | Mar–May | New tool install, hand-over countdown | Emotional blackmail, We're a family, "always on call" culture | PE + Install (Mia) |
| 6 | Jun–Jul | Mid-year review | Gaslighting, Emotional blackmail, Silent treatment | PE |
| 7 | August | Mentoring the new hire, Yu | Will you repeat all of it? | PE |

### Chapter 1 · First night shift (week 2)

- **Case:** SPC throws an OOC in the middle of the night and you nervously hold three lots. It turns out the metrology tool's calibration drifted and the process was fine all along. First lesson: check the measurement before you blame the process.
- **Story:**
  - Manager Wang gives you every night shift for the month: "More practice for you. It's for your own good." [For your own good]
  - Your incident report bounces three times for "wrong format", and nobody gives you a template. [Criticism without standards]
  - The fourth version follows his notes exactly. "That's not what I wanted. I need root-cause data." [Moving the goalposts]
  - Fang-jie quietly sends you the template everyone actually uses.
- **Choice:** ask Wang to put the report standard in an email (boundary, paper trail), or keep guessing.

### Chapter 2 · The night of Case 03 (10/21)

- **Case:** the current prototype. ETCH-07 PM-B's focus ring reaches end of life and pushes edge CD high.
- **Story:** at the 8 a.m. stand-up the next morning, Manager Wang reports to Director Chen: "I identified it as a focus ring issue, and it's been handled." You quietly mention that you found it.

> **Manager Wang:** "Didn't I tell you to check the focus ring yesterday? You must be remembering it wrong."

- [Tactics: Credit stealing, Gaslighting]
- **Choices (shaped by your Evidence Log):**
  - Pull up the LINE timestamps on the spot → the truth comes out, but Wang is humiliated in front of everyone. Relationship −−, Sanity +
  - After the meeting, email Wang a clean timeline and CC yourself → (recommended) professional, on the record. Relationship holds, Sanity +
  - Let it go → Sanity −, and it will happen again

### Chapter 3 · The blame game (Nov–Dec)

- **Case:** particle excursions hit WET-02 and the ETCH area at the same time. PE says it's the tool; EE says it's the process.
- **Guest gameplay:** play as equipment engineer Bear and fix the tool ("Tool Down!" gameplay: alarm, manual, parts swap, recovery).
- **Story:**
  - Manager Wang tells you: "Bear says you PEs open tickets for nothing."
  - Then he tells Bear: "The new hire says you EEs don't do any work."
  - [Tactic: Triangulation]
  - When you and Bear finally sit down with the data together, the culprit is the DI water line, and neither PE nor EE caused it.
- **Outcome:** Bear becomes an ally. Lesson: getting all three parties in a room with the data breaks triangulation.

### Chapter 4 · Empty promises (Jan–Feb)

- **Case:** a new product's yield ramp. You design experiments (DOE) to push yield from 70% to 90% in one month.
- **Story:**
  - At the company year-end party, Manager Wang, a few drinks in, throws an arm around your shoulder: "You did great this year. Your bonus won't disappoint. Next year you'll own ETCH-07." [Empty promises]
  - A month later, the KPIs change: "We want yield, not just stability." [Moving the goalposts]
  - "The new hires at the fab next door are already leading projects." [Comparison]
  - Kevin, drunk, tells you he heard the exact same promise three years ago.

### Chapter 5 · Expansion (Mar–May)

- **Case:** three new tools are moving in, and the hand-over date is fixed.
- **Guest gameplay:** play as install engineer Mia ("Hand-Over Countdown" gameplay: move-in, sub-fab piping puzzles, power-up, SAT checklist).
- **Story:**
  - "Nobody takes leave before hand-over. You really want to ask?" [We're a family]
  - "If you take leave, everyone else has to carry your load." [Emotional blackmail]
  - After back-to-back shifts, Kevin collapses in the fab and is taken to the hospital (he's going to be okay).
- **Choices:**
  - Cover Kevin's shifts → Sanity −−
  - Take the schedule straight to Director Chen → high risk
  - Push for a fair rotation together with Fang-jie, Bear, and Mia → requires enough allies

### Chapter 6 · Review (Jun–Jul)

- **Story:** the mid-year review. You bring up the promise from the year-end party.

> **Manager Wang:** "When did I ever say you'd own the tool? Had a bit too much to drink, did you?" [Gaslighting]
> **Manager Wang:** "Honestly? With your skills, no other company would hire you." [Emotional blackmail]

- For the next two weeks he stops inviting you to meetings and stops answering your messages. [Silent treatment, Isolation]
- **Turn:** in the pantry, you overhear Director Chen on the phone tearing into Manager Wang, worse than anything Wang ever said to you. Wang hangs up and stares out the window for a long time.
- A-Zhe tells you privately: "We're hiring at the OEM, you know."
- **Choices:**
  - File a complaint with HR using your Evidence Log (requires records from Chapters 1 and 2)
  - Request an internal transfer
  - Start sending out your résumé
  - Keep enduring

### Chapter 7 · The new hire (August)

- Yu starts, and you're the mentor now. On day one Yu asks: "Is it okay if I ask you about this?"
- Your dialogue options are every line you heard this year, next to the other way of saying it you learned:

| What you heard | What you could say instead |
|---|---|
| "If you don't know, go ask." | "If you don't know, ask me. Tell me what you already checked first." |
| "You need to ask about *this*?" | "Good question. I got stuck on that too." |
| "New hires don't need to rush off." | "That's enough for today. We'll pick it up tomorrow." |
| "Kevin knew this in his first week." | "You've come further than I had in my first week." |

- The choices in this chapter decide the ending.

## 7. Endings

| Ending | Condition | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Burnout** | Sanity hits zero at any point | One night shift, you sit on the bench in the gowning room and can't make yourself go back in. The narration is gentle: "This isn't your fault. Take a rest." Not a mocking game over |
| **The Cycle** | In Chapter 7 you repeat most of the tactics to Yu | Three years later, you're in Manager Wang's chair, telling a new hire: "If you don't know, go ask." |
| **Boundaries** | Enough sanity, boundaries set, evidence kept | You stay and do good work. Wang hasn't changed, but he stops trying it on you. Yu has someone to learn from |
| **Exit** | Choose the résumé route in Chapter 6 with enough sanity | You leave for somewhere better, carrying a year of experience and your Evidence Log. End card: "Leaving is not failing." |
| **Hidden: Pay It Forward** | High trust with every ally, every tactic recognized | With Fang-jie, Bear, Kevin, and Mia you build a real rotation and a real mentoring program. End card: "If you don't know, ask. I'll show you." |

## 8. Story systems

| System | Description |
|---|---|
| **Sanity (0–100)** | Drops from manipulation, back-to-back night shifts, and being yelled at. Restored by recognizing tactics, support from allies, and leaving on time. Zero triggers the Burnout ending |
| **Tactic Recognition** | When a manipulative line appears, the player can tag it with a Codex card. A correct tag halves that sanity loss and unlocks the card's explanation and healthy response |
| **Evidence Log** | Save conversations, messages, and meetings as timestamped records. Used for the confrontation in Chapter 2 and the HR complaint in Chapter 6 |
| **Response options** | Every manipulation event offers four responses: Swallow it / Clarify the facts / Set a boundary / Talk it over with someone. Each affects sanity and relationships |
| **Relationships** | One trust value per NPC. Affects whether people actually help when you ask, whether you can push the rotation in Chapter 5, and the hidden ending |
| **Coffee** | Already in the prototype. Short-term stamina, separate from sanity |

## 9. Tactics Codex (13 cards)

Compiled from articles on workplace manipulation ("workplace PUA"), emotional blackmail, and gaslighting (see section 10). In the game, each card includes "What this is" and "What you can do".

| # | Card | Line in the game | Chapter | What you can do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Double bind | "If you don't know, go ask" + "You need to ask about *this*?" + "Why didn't you ask?" | Prologue | Say what you already checked before asking; ask your manager to name who you can go to |
| 2 | Criticism without standards | "Wrong format. Redo it." (no template) | 1 | Ask for a template or concrete criteria, confirmed in writing |
| 3 | Personal attack | "What did they teach you in school?" "How can you be so careless?" | Prologue, 6 | Take only the work-related part (separate their issue from yours) |
| 4 | Moving the goalposts | "That's not what I wanted." (the bar keeps changing) | 1, 4 | Write the goal down and reconfirm whenever it changes |
| 5 | For your own good | "Night shifts are extra practice for you." | 1 | Judge the workload against the pay, not the packaging |
| 6 | We're a family | "We're family. Nobody takes leave before hand-over." | Prologue, 5 | Work is a contract. Family doesn't schedule you like this |
| 7 | Comparison | "Kevin knew this in his first week." | Prologue, 4 | Go back to your own standard and your own progress record |
| 8 | Credit stealing | Presenting your finding as his at the stand-up | 2 | Email a timeline after the meeting so the work is traceable |
| 9 | Gaslighting | "Didn't I tell you to check it? You're remembering it wrong." | 2, 6 | Record things as they happen, and trust your records |
| 10 | Triangulation | "Bear says you PEs open tickets for nothing." | 3 | Get all three parties in a room with the data |
| 11 | Empty promises | "Your bonus won't disappoint. Next year you'll own the tool." | 4 | A promise counts when it's in writing |
| 12 | Emotional blackmail | "If you take leave, everyone carries your load." "No other company would hire you." | 5, 6 | Stop, look, respond: don't answer right away, check whether it's really your responsibility, then reply |
| 13 | Silent treatment and isolation | No replies, no meeting invites, no feedback | 6 | Ask for feedback directly, and build support outside the department |

**Writing rules:**
- Codex explanations must be accurate and must not lecture. Before release, have them reviewed by mental-health or labor professionals.
- Include help resources in the game (complaint channels, counseling). Verify them per region before release.
- "Workplace PUA" is a popular term, not a legal or medical one. Codex wording must not suggest the player can use it to convict anyone.
